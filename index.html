About

The AICS Parliamentary Open 2026 is a full‑day British Parliamentary debate tournament hosted at the Amsterdam International Community School (AICS) on Saturday, 14 November 2026 . The event brings together 32 teams from international, bilingual, and gymnasium schools across the Netherlands for a day of competitive debating, workshops, and adjudication led by experienced judges from National and University debating societies Across the Netherlands.

Event Details

  • Date: Saturday The 14th Of November

  • Time: 10:00-18:00

  • Location: Bijlmerplein 1000, 1102 MK Amsterdam (South East)

  • Format: British Parliamentary

Our Mission

Our mission is to give middle school and high school students across the Netherlands a platform to access high-quality, professionally adjudicated competitive debating more often. We strongly believe every young debater deserves the opportunity to compete more often, and improve their public speaking skills.

Our Vision

We at the AICS Debate Club strongly belive that British Parliamentary debating equips students with the public speaking skills that they will need for their life. Our vision is to grow English-Language debating across the Netherlands, building a stronger, more connected community of young debaters nationwide.

Partners & Sponsors

The Nederlandse Debatbond (NDB) is the official Dutch National Debate Association. Serving as a national umbrella organization, it represents roughly 1,000 members across the Netherlands, most of whom belong to university debating societies. The NDB supports the community by organizing national championships, managing rankings, and fostering competitive debate nationwide.

The Oracy Institute is an educational organization that trains students and teachers in public speaking, competitive debate, and critical thinking across both English and Dutch. Led by championship-winning competitive debaters, the institute helps youth develop Public Speaking & Crticial Thinking Skills through school curriculum, teacher training programs, and specialized debate clubs.

Founded in 1989, the Erasmus Debating Society (EDS) is the official debate club of Erasmus University Rotterdam and the oldest, largest, and most prestigious university debating society in the Netherlands. This highly international club expertly balances elite competitive championship success with a thriving, vibrant, and welcoming social scene for all of its members.

ASDV Bonaparte is Amsterdam’s premier student debating society, bringing together a vibrant mix of Dutch and international students every Tuesday. This welcoming, hazing-free community is the ultimate launchpad to master public speaking, perfectly pairing competitive tournament success with an unforgettable social calendar of weekly drinks, trips, and elegant galas.

Registration

Register

To register for the competition, each team (consisting of two members) must fill in the team registration form. In order to contribute towards all of the expenses regarding the tournament, we have a registration fee of 15 euros per person. The AICS Parliamentary Open has a maximum capacity of 32 teams. Any teams that register after this count will be placed on a waiting list until we are able to confirm additional capacity for judges who are able to accommodate the remaining teams.

Please note that there is a registration fees of €15 per participant & the registration form must be filled out per team

The deadline for signing up is the 10th of November 2026 12:00 PM
